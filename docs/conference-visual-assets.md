# Conference landing page visual assets

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 24 September 2026. Final WebP assets are in `public/Confeernce system/landing/`. Images illustrate meeting environments; catalogue product cards continue to use the original product photographs.

## Prompt set

All generated assets use photorealistic commercial photography, pale white/blue and navy colours, no embedded website text, no watermarks, and no added logos.

- **hero.webp**: 3:1 banner using the original conference banner as the device reference. Preserve the four microphone designs and rack controller; place devices on the right 55% of a marble boardroom table. Daylit city windows, black chairs and charcoal wall behind. Left 43% bright empty space for live HTML copy.
- **wired.webp**: 2:1 card using the original wired conference device image. Preserve the two microphones, remove rack controller, place products lower right on a white table in a bright neutral boardroom. Left 52% empty white fade.
- **wireless.webp**: 2:1 card using the original wireless conference device image. Preserve the two wireless microphone designs, remove controller and rear panel, place products lower right in an airy boardroom with tall windows and grey chairs. Left 52% empty white fade.
- **integration.webp**: 3:2 white meeting room showing a PTZ camera, wall display with remote presenter, one conference controller and three gooseneck microphones based on the supplied wired product reference. Thin cyan connection lines illustrate integration; no labels or headings.
- **government.webp**: Formal government meeting room, symmetrical polished wooden conference table, tabletop microphones, black chairs, blue presentation display, contemporary European interior and daylight.
- **council.webp**: Municipal council chamber with curved rows of wooden desks, individual conference microphones, upholstered seats, classical windows and no people.
- **education.webp**: Bright university seminar room with rows of light tables, desktop microphones, cream walls, tall windows and a professional presentation display.
- **training.webp**: Corporate training centre with classroom tables, black chairs, blue presentation display, discreet conference microphones and white/wood finishes.
- **hall.webp**: Large conference hall with curved wooden acoustic ceiling, rows of seats and discussion desks, warm architectural lighting.
- **international.webp**: Milan business skyline with contemporary glass towers, daylight blue sky and realistic corporate architecture.

Application tiles use horizontal 3:2 compositions. Existing meeting-room and control-room photographs are reused where they match the reference. `world-map.svg` uses the existing project map geometry.

Card edit prompt: preserve both devices, move their complete silhouettes into the rightmost 45% and lower 75% of the 2:1 image, leave the left 53% empty for HTML copy, retain the bright meeting room, never crop a microphone, no added text or logos.
